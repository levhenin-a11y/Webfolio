I Am
Vincent Henin... and Vhenin

Hello, I was born in Brussels in 1979.
I signed 4 comics and collaborated to others under my 'real name', Vincent Henin, published by Casterman. Under the pseudonym Vhenin, I created 2 comics of the duo 'Blateman & Bobine' with the scenarist Tarek. But not only...

At the same time, I am pursuing a career in (Motion) Graphic Design, Illustration, Webdesign and as a Interface Designer. Recently, I also offer services like Graphic Recording and Visual Thinking for clients such as the European Commission, the 'EU DataViz, The European Convention Center (Luxembourg), Trees & People and many others.

It is to offer these services independently that my 'Vhenin' freelance activity was born.
Discover both sides on this site.

Comics / Voyages d'Alix

Artistic director: Jacques Martin / Category: Historical and didactic comic book / 56 pages / Casterman publishing

Texts-Drawings-Colours: Vincent Henin

Even if Carthage was destroyed by the Romans at the end of the Punic Wars, to be rebuilt later as a Roman colony, it is in any case fully reconstructed in its architecture and history throughout this book. Let Alix guide you...

 

Voyages d'Alix (Journeys of Alix) - Jerusalem

Comics / Voyages d'Alix

Artistic director: Jacques Martin / Category: Historical and didactic comic book / 56 pages / Casterman publishing

Texts-Drawings-Colours: Vincent Henin

The city of peace, always vibrant and current, is revealed throughout the journey of Alix and Enak. From its foundation directly linked to the Jewish people until its annexation to the Roman Empire. Discover its history, its architecture and its great kings... At the end of the book, the important sites to visit if you go to Israel.

I work with them

I am part of a Collective of Visual Thinkers

"Visual Thinkers?!" But, what is that?

This term encompasses several techniques of graphic capture, organization and storage: Graphic Recording, Visual Recording and Harvesting alongside Sketch Noting.

"A drawing is better than a long speech", right? Well, we appeal to your cognitive, creative and visual abilities to create, nourish and retain the words of meetings, debates or events... in drawings

You finally have before your eyes the drawn or graphically transposed report of these. And in a more durable and clear way for lasting and constructive memorization... Also, this constitutes a more user-friendly material to use, to share, to remember and to revive the discussion between colleagues later.



Go to "VisualThinkers.eu"!
